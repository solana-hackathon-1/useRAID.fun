## Outcome and evidence boundary

The repository contains a social campaign product: creators publish campaigns, participants enroll and submit X posts, tracking produces reviewed snapshots, and SOL/USDC flows account for rewards. These are reuse candidates, not implementations of every Guild/work contract in the blueprint.

The inspected schema has **35 models and 18 migrations**. It contains no `Participant`, `Guild`, immutable work `TermsVersion`, `Engagement`, global `ActiveCommitment`, asset registry, generic `CommandReceipt`, or resource `AccessGrant` model. Existing `RaidEnrollment` uniqueness is per campaign, not global capacity. A campaign's `ACTIVE` status and `maxActiveRaids` are not a participant's one-work-slot guarantee.

No approved deployment snapshot, live database, custody balance or provider state was inspected. Counts of actual unpaid obligations and distributions of stored policy versions are **NOT VERIFIED**. Test fixtures cannot establish those counts. No policies were changed or adopted, no migrations applied, and no production gates approved.

Authority is [the canonical blueprint](../../RAID_AGENT_BLUEPRINT.md), with [the section map](../../RAID_BLUEPRINT_MAP.md), [numbered contracts](../raid-agent-task-contracts.md) and [module reuse contract](../raid-module-reuse.md). Classification remains: LOCKED R01–R16; INVARIANT I01–I13; DIRECTION P01–P08; DEFAULT D01–D13; OPEN O01–O19. Examples remain illustrative. The code's existing provider choices, fees, limits and deadlines are observed implementation facts; they do not resolve OPEN decisions without an accepted section 40.4 record. The accepted 6 October TDD/reuse decisions remain intact.

## Concrete keys, tenant scope and authority

Most entities use CUIDs; provider posts use provider IDs, tokens use mint strings, wallets use addresses. Preserve those identifiers rather than replacing them with example UUIDs. Relations generally restrict deletion of campaign/money history; session/wallet/X-account children cascade from User, and media uploader deletion sets null. Several semantic links (`Raid.activeResultId`, `RaidDispute.resultId`, some round/launch finance references) are plain strings rather than declared FKs. T003 must inspect these and orphan counts before strengthening constraints.

Existing resource scope is creator-owned Raid plus platform administrator, not Guild tenancy. [Admin authorization](../../src/lib/admins.ts) requires role ADMIN, unsuspended user and environment X-ID allowlist (isolated Studio fixtures have a test-only exception); sensitive route access also requires an X-issued session. This is not the scoped support/reviewer/treasury separation required for new domains.
