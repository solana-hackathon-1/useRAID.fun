# Consented amendments and replacement contracts

T025 lets accepted work change only by agreement and lets a terminated worker be replaced without rewriting history. The Founder proposes a change; nothing is effective until the affected member consents to that exact version, and any added reward is separately reserved first. A terminated attempt is replaced by a new slot with its own terms and reservation, linked to the attempt it follows. All funding is simulated. Root routes and screens, notifications, disputes, settlement, withdrawal, multi-member rosters and reopening terminal work remain separate work.

Known limits from the review (#244): one member's consent suffices because only solo rosters are implemented; database guards cannot bind consent to the digest or check funding; the consent-versus-submission race test asserts an either/or outcome rather than a barrier-controlled interleaving. At consent the proposer's Founder authority is asked of the host again (empty `sessionHash`) and a lapsed proposer, expired proposal or lapsed amended deadline is `AMENDMENT_UNAVAILABLE`; a slot or earlier-amendment funding reference is `FUNDING_REQUIRED`; migration `202610270002_funding_reference_serialization` serializes slot-versus-amendment reuse of one reference with a per-reference advisory lock.

Requirement mapping: T025 uses AG03/AG02, M2, R04/R13, I05/I08/I10, AC25/AC72 and blueprint §§11.5, 12.1–12.5, 13, 16 (no money moves), 17, 24.6–24.7, 25–28. Full numbered task criteria, Root integration, UAT, business/operating/release approval and withdrawal (no withdrawal command exists) remain NOT VERIFIED; roster/member replacement inside one multi-member engagement, extensions after submission and consent for non-HUMAN participants are not implemented. This bounded implementation does not close the issue.

## Public module interface

`amendment-storage.ts` exports `proposeAmendment`, `respondToAmendment` (`ACCEPT`/`DECLINE`), `withdrawAmendment`, `readEffectiveTerms` (member) and `readEngagementAmendments` (member or authorized Founder). `replacement-storage.ts` exports `reissueRaid(tx, WorkCommand, WorkHost)` with `{engagementId, terms}`. Errors add `AMENDMENT_EXISTS` (one open proposal), `AMENDMENT_UNAVAILABLE` (decided, withdrawn or expired), `CONSENT_REQUIRED`, `REPLACEMENT_EXISTS`; wrong state is `ENGAGEMENT_STATE`; unauthorized and missing are one `FORBIDDEN`/`NOT_FOUND`. Amendments are only accepted while the engagement is `ACCESS_PENDING` or `IN_PROGRESS`: submitted, closed or terminated work is never re-scoped. Events (`work.amendment_{proposed,accepted,declined,withdrawn}.v1`, `work.raid_reissued.v1`) carry identifiers only; `amendmentId` is an additive optional envelope key.

## State, locking and database enforcement

Lock order is receipt → actor identity (SHARE) → Engagement `FOR UPDATE` → amendment `FOR UPDATE`, matching submission, review and closure, so consent racing a submission yields exactly one winner. Migration `202610260006_work_amendments` adds `EngagementAmendment` (one open proposal per engagement, RESTRICT FKs, `proposedByAccountId`/`decidedByAccountId` classified in the identity fence), insert and update guards (only amendable fields change; reward legs, policy and beneficiary fixed; increase needs a funding reference; deadlines ordered and in the future; only a member may accept or decline, only the proposer withdraws; an accepted row needs consent), `RaidReplacement` (one per terminated engagement; the replacement must be a distinct, funded, open slot of the same Founder and Guild) and replaces the submission, review and closure guards so they read effective deadlines. TypeScript readers (submission, review criteria, closure claims, workspace) use `raid_effective_terms`. Backup and restore keep amendments with engagements; destructive down migration is unsupported once used.

## Operation and verification

Monitor open proposals near expiry, `CONSENT_REQUIRED`/`FUNDING_REQUIRED` rates and reservations without an accepted amendment (an unaccepted reservation is not released here; release belongs to the funding/settlement task). `tests/work-amendments-db.test.ts` verifies: terms unchanged until consent then versioned with funding, consent-gated criteria/reward/deadline flow into submission, review and claims, unilateral Founder/admin/stranger/member/listing-edit/direct-SQL rejection, funding and consent failures, decline/withdraw/expiry/replay/one-open-proposal, malformed and immutable-field input, submitted and closed work refused, a consent-versus-submission race with one outcome, and replacement of a truly terminated worker with a new slot, terms and reservation while the original claim stays visible and unsettled, plus unauthorized, early and disabled replacement. Test names and genuine RED/GREEN results belong in the PR; independent review disposition is recorded there separately from SELF-EVALUATED author checks.
