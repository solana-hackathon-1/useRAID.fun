# Product activation and repeat metrics (operator)

`GET /api/v1/admin/metrics/product` gives an authorized operator an honest, read-only view of who activates and who returns in the useful work loop, on simulated funding. Every ratio states its numerator, denominator, denominator definition and exclusions. It is private and default off, and it complements the per-state and per-asset [work-loop metrics](raid-work-metrics.md) rather than replacing them.

## Contract and requirements

| Requirement / measure                                    | Behavior / verification                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Blueprint §31.1 Guild activation                         | Numerator: persisted (operator-activated) Guilds with a published slot holding a simulated funding reference. Denominator: all persisted Guilds. `timings.guildToFirstFundedOffering` is Guild creation to the first such slot.                                                                              |
| §31.1 First-Raid completion                              | `guildFirstOutcome`: Guilds with at least one engagement closed as work accepted over all persisted Guilds. `timings.guildToFirstOutcome` is Guild creation to that closure. Work accepted is not payment.                                                                                                   |
| §31.1 Raider activation and time to first useful outcome | `raiderActivation`: participants who hold an engagement over participants who applied to, or are members of an engagement on, a verified-Guild slot. `raiderFirstOutcome`: with a work-accepted engagement over those who hold one. `timings.raiderToFirstOutcome` is participant creation to first closure. |
| §31.1 Repeat Guilds/contributors                         | `guildRepeat` and `raiderRepeat`: two or more work-accepted engagements over one or more. This is a **DEFAULT** reading of "returning for another valid engagement", not owner policy (`definitions: DEFAULT_NOT_OWNER_POLICY`).                                                                             |
| §31.2 reporting integrity, I03                           | Counts and durations only: no amount, asset total, valuation or dollar field exists, so nothing can be summed across assets or presented as paid. The strict schema rejects any extra field a host might add. Per-asset volumes remain in the work-loop metrics.                                             |
| §31.2 exclusions                                         | Work under a Guild id with no persisted Guild is excluded from every figure and counted in `excluded`. Fixtures, internal demonstrations, duplicates and known abuse are **not marked in the data**, so they are not excluded; each metric says so and `notMeasured` names them.                             |
| Honest absence                                           | A zero denominator gives `rate: null`, an empty timing gives `null` median/p90 with `samples: 0`; a numerator above its denominator or a rate that disagrees is a schema error (503).                                                                                                                        |
| I10, I06, §28                                            | Operator only: the shared administrator rule (`requireUser(true)`), a per-operator rate limit, `private, no-store`, query strings and non-GET refused, flag exactly `PRODUCT_METRICS_READS_ENABLED=true` (else 404 before authorization or any read). No accounts, handles, brief text or evidence appear.   |
| R15/R16                                                  | `src/metrics/{contracts,storage,http}` are portable; hosts inject the flag, operator authority, rate limit and transaction. The independent [metrics host](../examples/metrics/README.md) reads the report over its own minimal tables and checks the honesty rules.                                         |

Requirement mapping: T043 (activation/repeat part), AG03, blueprint §§24.6-24.7, 28, 31.1-31.2. Not built here, and NOT VERIFIED: launch conversion, settlement completion, dispute burden, private-access reliability, AI and promotion measures, review delay excluding documented pauses, opportunity concentration and other §31.3 fairness inspection, fixture/abuse/duplicate exclusion markers, dated approved price methodology and any dollar value, a public success claim, analytics events and surveys. Full numbered task criteria, UAT and business/release approval remain NOT VERIFIED. The ratio definitions above are defaults for operators, not an owner-approved metric policy.

## Host contract

`PRODUCT_METRICS_READS_ENABLED` is an exact-string `true` flag, default off, independent of `WORK_METRICS_READS_ENABLED`. `authorize` must resolve only for an authorized operator and return their account id (it rejects with `MetricsHttpError` 401/403); `rateLimit(operatorAccountId)` rejects with 429. A response outside the strict `ProductMetricsSchema` is `503` and never forwarded. The storage module reads existing tables (`Guild`, `Participant`, `WorkListing`, `RaidSlot`, `RaidApplication`, `Engagement`, `EngagementMember`, `EngagementClosure`) through an injected READ COMMITTED PostgreSQL transaction and performs no write.

## Verification

`tests/metrics-product-db.test.ts` (real PostgreSQL: empty platform, three Guilds and four participants with activation, first outcome, repeat, exclusion of unverified-Guild work, denominators and no money/account fields), `-http` (flag, authority and throttling before reading, query/method/contract drift, inconsistent ratios, per-operator throttle identity) and `-host` (exact flag independent of the work-loop flag, shared administrator rule). The `examples/metrics` host exercises the same modules over its own schema.
